# WO-10552 — CNC-03 Spindle Vibration: Bearing Failure (Lubrication Root Cause)

**Work order:** WO-10552  
**Asset:** CNC-03, LINE-01  
**Date opened:** 2025-06-14 06:42  
**Date closed:** 2025-06-14 10:18  
**Technician:** R. Okonkwo (Tech ID: TECH-219)  
**Shift:** Day shift — Supervisor: M. Alvarez  
**Classification:** Corrective maintenance — spindle  
**Total downtime:** 3 hours 36 minutes  

---

## 1. Symptom report

Operator T. Singh reported rising spindle vibration on CNC-03 at 06:42 during production of Gear Assembly part number GA-4471. Vibration reading at time of report: **4.8 mm/s RMS** (normal operating band: ≤ 2.5 mm/s). Cycle time was also slightly elevated at 47 s (baseline 42 s). Defect rate at time of report was within normal range at 1.8 %.

Machine was stopped at next part completion (06:51) per LINE-01 SOP MFG-SOP-LINE01-007 Tier 2 procedure. Suspect parts flagged from serial GA-4471-1843 onwards (14 parts held for quality inspection).

---

## 2. Diagnostic sequence

### Step 1 — Tool and tool-holder inspection (07:05–07:25)

Tool holder removed and inspected. Taper contact surface: 91 % blued contact — within acceptance. Balance class: G2.5 at 25,000 RPM — correct specification. Runout at 50 mm gauge length: 0.0024 mm TIR — within 0.003 mm acceptance limit. **Tool holder cleared.**

### Step 2 — Spindle bearing condition (07:25–08:10)

Spindle rotated by hand: intermittent roughness detected at approximately 90° intervals — consistent with ball damage. Accelerometer mounted at front bearing housing. Spectrum showed:

- Elevated broadband noise floor above 2 kHz: **+8 dB above last baseline** (taken 2025-05-28).
- Ball-pass frequency (outer race): strong harmonic at 287 Hz and 3rd order.
- 1× runout: 0.009 mm — elevated vs baseline of 0.003 mm.

Bearing condition: **confirmed degraded — replacement required.**

### Step 3 — Lubricator inspection (08:10–08:25)

Checked oil-air mist lubricator at front spindle bearing port. **Flow indicator red — no oil flow.** Filter (part BIJUR-F-50) found blocked with coolant contamination. This is the confirmed root cause of accelerated bearing wear: bearing ran dry for an estimated 40–60 operating hours before the vibration threshold was reached.

---

## 3. Corrective action

1. Replaced front spindle bearing (SKF-7018-BECBP, from Stores bin B-14). Installation torque: 85 Nm per OEM specification. Used thermal induction heater — bearing temperature at installation: 110 °C.
2. Replaced blocked lubricator filter (BIJUR-F-50) with new unit from Maintenance Store M-03. Primed oil-air mist system and confirmed green flow indicator.
3. Cleaned spindle nose and housing with lint-free cloth. No corrosion found.
4. Performed spindle warm-up cycle per OEM procedure (10 min at 1,000 RPM, 10 min at 5,000 RPM, 10 min at 12,000 RPM).

---

## 4. Verification

Post-repair vibration reading at 12,000 RPM (production spindle speed): **0.9 mm/s RMS** — within normal operating band.  
Runout at 50 mm gauge length: 0.0018 mm TIR — improved vs pre-failure baseline.  
Cycle time on first production part: 42 s — at baseline.  
Quality inspection of held parts (14 units): all within tolerance — released for production.

---

## 5. Lessons learned and recommendations

**Critical lesson:** The bearing failure was not caused by natural wear — it was caused by a blocked lubricator filter that had not been checked during the previous 500-hour preventive maintenance (PM) visit. Review of PM records confirmed the lubricator filter was not on the PM-500 checklist for CNC-03. This gap has been escalated to Reliability Engineering.

**Action items raised:**
- **Action 1 (TECH-219):** Add lubricator filter inspection and replacement to PM-500 checklist for CNC-03, CNC-04, and CNC-07. Target completion: 2025-06-21.
- **Action 2 (Reliability Engineering):** Assess whether oil-air mist flow sensor can be connected to MES for automatic alert on zero-flow condition. Reference: similar implementation on LINE-03 press line.
- **Action 3 (Maintenance Planning):** Schedule PM-500 for CNC-03 in 480 operating hours from today (estimated date: 2025-09-08).

**For future incidents:** When CNC-03 vibration rises gradually over several weeks and reaches the warning threshold, consider lubricator condition as a probable cause alongside bearing wear — especially if no tooling changes were made in the period. This failure mode is distinguishable from tool-holder imbalance by the absence of dimensional drift in quality data.
